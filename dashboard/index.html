Pre-token Radar

Protocols with no token on record, ranked by what one would be worth

Implied value = what a ridge regression fitted on the protocols whose token already trades says this protocol’s market cap would be, from its own TVL, years listed and category (7-day TVL change was tested and dropped: it did not lower the held-out miss). Every figure comes from the sealed DefiLlama snapshot. A model, not a promise.
